About Emarat

For four decades, Emarat has brought energy to everyday life. From quality fuel to a familiar welcome, we are here for our communities and every journey ahead.

Quality fuel, everyday essentials and service you can rely on. A familiar part of life across Dubai and the Northern Emirates.
